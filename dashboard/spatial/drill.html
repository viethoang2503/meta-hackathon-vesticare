Voice drill for M7

Opens an incident by hand so the phone's voice path can be measured without a fall. Nothing is sent to Telegram. Results are labelled “Voice drill (no device, no fall detected)”.

1. Session

Then open the phone page: run .verify/venv/bin/python backend/tools/wearer_link.py --tunnel https://<tunnel> and scan the QR with iPhone 1.

2. One trial per press

Press, wait for the phone to ask “Are you all right?”, then do what the button says.

No session

3. Speaker echo trials (nobody answers)

Does the phone hear its own voice? Pick the phone's speaker volume and the room, then press a button and stay silent. Do each button at least 3 times at each of low, mid and high. The window must be at least 30 s for the first button. Audio is not stored; transcripts are.
